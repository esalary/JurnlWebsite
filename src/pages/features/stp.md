Jurnl makes your Single Touch Payroll file from each pay date and keeps a full STP Phase 2 breakdown of what everyone has been paid this year.

## What you get

- **The Single Touch Lite file** for each pay date, with year-to-date amounts and termination codes.
- **STP breakdown:** year to date per person the way STP Phase 2 reports it: gross, overtime, bonuses, directors' fees, allowances by type, paid leave by type, salary sacrifice, lump sums, ETPs, deductions and super.
- **Tax treatment codes** worked out from each person's tax details.
- **Checks:** the breakdown confirms its parts add up to gross pay, and flags anything missing, such as a working holiday maker's home country.
- **Save it as CSV.**

## Lodging

You lodge the Single Touch Lite file through your usual channel. Lodging directly from Jurnl is planned and waiting on ATO software registration.
