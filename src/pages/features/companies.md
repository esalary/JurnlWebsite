## Several companies

Add as many companies as you need, each with its own folder, people, chart of accounts and branding.

- **A setup wizard** for a new company: name, ABN, GST registration, bank details and pay schedules.
- **Switch companies** from the user menu.
- **Registered for GST or not:** when not, invoices, bills and the BAS leave GST out.
- **Package pay or standard pay** chosen per company.

## Your branding

- Upload a logo (PNG, JPG or PDF) and pick your colours; a preview shows an invoice before you save.
- Your logo and colours appear on invoices, credit notes, pay slips and reports.

## Moving in

Import from Jurnl's Excel templates, preview every change, and apply only what you choose.

- **Templates** for employees, customers and suppliers, with notes on every column.
- **A preview** showing new records, changed fields (old to new, with sensitive values masked) and errors such as an invalid TFN, ABN or BSB.
- **Update or skip** each matching record.
- **"What's new"** after each upgrade, so you know what changed.
