Make a read-only copy for one person, and they open it in Jurnl with their own password. They don't need a cloud drive account, and they don't need a licence to view it.

## What goes in the copy

- **Made by the owner for a named person,** as one encrypted file you can email or share by link.
- **You choose each time:** include payroll or leave it out (the accounts still show total wages, PAYG and super), and show employee names or payroll IDs only.
- **Personal and bank details are always removed:** tax file numbers, dates of birth, addresses, phone numbers, super member numbers and bank details.

## Opening it

- **One button on the start screen,** with step-by-step instructions. The copy shows who made it and the date its figures are from.
- **Ready-to-paste instructions** for your email are made with the copy.
- **Nothing is ever written back** to your books, and opening a newer copy replaces the old one.
- **Viewing a copy is free:** no licence, and it doesn't count towards company or employee limits.

## If they don't use Jurnl

Email them the [accountant pack](/features/bas-and-reports/) instead: profit and loss, balance sheet, trial balance, general ledger, GST and PAYG by quarter and a payroll summary, as PDFs and one Excel workbook, with employees shown by payroll ID only.
