Branded tax invoices and credit notes, emailed from your own mailbox, with payments tracked against each one.

## Invoices

- **Tax invoices** with your logo and colours, numbered in sequence and never reused (a void keeps its number).
- **Lines** with quantity, rate, income account and tax code: GST, GST free, export or input taxed.
- **Edit freely until it's emailed;** after that a change is an amendment with a reason, and the earlier PDF is kept.
- **Copy an invoice,** or save one as a template with placeholders for the month and year.
- **Invoices list** with what's owed, overdue flags, and filters by customer, date and status.

## Payments and credit notes

- **Record payments** without waiting for the bank statement, including part payments and refunds. When the statement arrives, its line takes the payment's place.
- **Credit notes** against an invoice reduce the sale, its GST and what's owed, with their own PDF.

## Customers

- Import customers from a QuickBooks contact list or Jurnl's own Excel template.
- For businesses that bill a client for their people's hours: invoices made straight from the pay run, one per person, kept in step with the hours. See [for salary packaging](/salary-packaging/).

## Sending

Email invoices through Microsoft 365 (drafts in your Outlook, or sent directly) or any SMTP server. Every email is recorded against its invoice, including failures, and you can change the wording of invoice emails, with placeholders.
