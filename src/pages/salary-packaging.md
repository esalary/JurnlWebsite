Jurnl was built for a salary packaging and contractor management business, so it handles the way those businesses pay people, which few small business products do.

## Pay on a package rate

- **The package** is the hours worked times the person's package rate.
- **Fees and insurance** come out of the package before gross, as a fixed amount or a percentage.
- **Super guarantee** is worked out from what's left, and salary sacrifice to super or other items (such as a car lease or work expenses) comes out before tax.
- **PAYG** is worked out on the taxable amount from the ATO's 2026–27 tables, and after-tax deductions such as a novated lease employee contribution come out of net pay.

## Invoice the client from the same run

For people placed with a client, Jurnl makes the invoices straight from the pay run: one per person, for their hours at the agreed rate plus GST, kept in step with the hours if the run changes.

## Everything else included

The same pay run posts the wages journal, PAYG, super and payments to the ledger, and the bank statement import matches the net pays, super and client receipts. Package pay or standard pay is chosen per company, so you can run both kinds of business in one app.
