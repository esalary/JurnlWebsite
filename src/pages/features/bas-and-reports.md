## Activity statement (BAS)

Every activity statement label from your books, on the basis you report to the ATO, with the transactions behind each figure.

- **GST:** G1 total sales, G2 exports, G3 GST-free sales, G10 capital purchases, G11 other purchases, 1A and 1B.
- **Tax codes** on every sale and purchase line drive the labels.
- **GST detail:** every transaction grouped by label, so you can check each figure.
- **PAYG withholding:** W1 and W2 from your approved pays, by month, quarter or year.
- **PAYG instalments:** by the ATO's rate (T1 × T2) or its quarterly amount, to 5A.
- **Whole dollars,** cents dropped as the ATO asks, beside the exact amounts.

You lodge your BAS with the ATO as usual.

## Accounting

A full double-entry ledger, posted automatically from your pay runs, invoices, bills and bank lines.

- **Chart of accounts** with four-digit codes, ready to use for a services business, and yours to change.
- **Journals** posted automatically and replaced when their source changes; manual journals for anything else.
- **Company expenses** entered in one go when you don't have a statement.
- **Accrual books,** with cash-basis reports at the flick of a switch.
- **Period lock:** nothing dated in a locked period can change.

## Reports

Profit and loss, balance sheet, trial balance and account transactions, as PDF or CSV.

- **Profit and loss:** single period with percentage of income, by month, against the previous period or last year; accrual or cash.
- **Balance sheet** with GST, retained earnings and current year earnings.
- **Trial balance** as at a date, or for a period with opening and closing balances.
- **Account transactions** with search; click any figure to see what's behind it.
- **Accountant pack:** everything for year end in one zip, PDFs and an Excel workbook, with employees shown by payroll ID only.
