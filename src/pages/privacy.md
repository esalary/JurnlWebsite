Jurnl is made by ESalary Pty Ltd (ABN 71 119 121 023). This policy covers the personal information we collect through this website, and explains how the Jurnl software handles your business data.

## Your business data stays with you

Jurnl is a desktop app. The books, payroll records and other data you enter are stored in an encrypted database on your own computers and in the shared folder you choose. We don't receive or hold that data.

## What we collect on this website

- **Free trial requests:** your name, email, business name if you give it, how many people you pay, and your computer type.
- **Enquiries:** your name, email and message.

This website doesn't use analytics or tracking cookies.

## Why we use it

To send you the trial, respond to your enquiry, and follow up about Jurnl. You can ask us to stop contacting you at any time. We never sell personal information.

## Storage and overseas

We keep enquiries and trial requests in our Microsoft 365 mailbox, which generally stores Australian customers' data in Australia. This website is hosted on Microsoft Azure, which may process website requests outside Australia.

## Access, correction and complaints

Email [privacy@jurnl.com.au](mailto:privacy@jurnl.com.au) to see or correct the personal information we hold about you, or to make a complaint. We'll respond within 30 days. If you're not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at [oaic.gov.au](https://www.oaic.gov.au).

## Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always on this page.
