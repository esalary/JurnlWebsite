Jurnl runs your pay runs, invoices, bills, bank reconciliation and activity statement from one place. Approving a pay run posts the wages journal; invoices, bills and bank lines post as you save them. The ledger, profit and loss, balance sheet and activity statement keep themselves up to date as you work.

## [Payroll and leave](/features/payroll/)

Pay runs that work out the tax for you: weekly, fortnightly or monthly, on wages or salary, with PAYG from the ATO's tables, leave that keeps its own balance, and final pays done right.

## [Single Touch Payroll](/features/stp/)

Your Single Touch Lite file from each pay date, and a full STP Phase 2 breakdown of what everyone has been paid this year.

## [Invoicing](/features/invoicing/)

Branded tax invoices and credit notes, emailed from your own mailbox, with payments tracked against each one.

## [Bills and bank](/features/bills-and-bank/)

Enter a bill once and pay it with a bank file. Import your bank statement and Jurnl matches each line to what you've already recorded.

## [BAS and reports](/features/bas-and-reports/)

Every activity statement label from your books, with the transactions behind each figure, plus a ledger that keeps itself up to date and the reports your accountant needs.

## [Companies and moving in](/features/companies/)

One app for every business you run, documents in your own branding, and imports that bring your people, customers and suppliers with you.

## [Security](/security/)

An encrypted database on your own computer, a sign-in for each person, and an audit trail of every change.

## Also included

- Several companies in one app, each with its own folder, people and branding.
- Your logo and colours on invoices, pay slips and reports.
- Email invoices and pay slips through Microsoft 365 or any SMTP mail server, as drafts to check or sent straight away.
- Lock the books once a quarter is reconciled, so nothing in it can change.
- An accountant pack in one zip: reports, general ledger, GST and PAYG by quarter and a payroll summary, with employees shown only by payroll ID.
- Upgrades back up your database first, every time.
