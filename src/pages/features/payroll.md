Pay staff weekly, fortnightly or monthly, on an hourly rate or an annual salary, with PAYG worked out from the ATO's tables.

## Pay runs

- **Pay schedules:** weekly, fortnightly (two offset fortnights if you need them) and monthly, each with its own pay date offset. Jurnl suggests the next period.
- **Hourly or salaried:** salary is divided by the pays in a year; hours per pay fill in automatically.
- **Pay items you set up:** overtime at time and a half or double time, allowances, bonuses, commission and directors' fees. Each says whether it is ordinary time earnings.
- **Super guarantee** at the current rate on ordinary time earnings, paid on top.
- **Deductions and salary sacrifice:** to super or other items, fixed amounts or a percentage, before or after tax.
- **Up to three bank accounts** per employee, split by amount or percentage.
- **Payroll notes:** write a reminder against a person and it appears as a checklist in their next pay run; open notes block approval.
- **Several batches** in one period for late starters or corrections.
- **Approve** to lock the run and post it to the ledger; reopen it if it hasn't been lodged.

## PAYG for every tax situation

Tax-free threshold claimed or not, no TFN, foreign resident, working holiday maker (registered employer or not), Medicare levy exemption or reduction, study and training loans, and a varied rate or extra amount. Withholding comes from the ATO's 2026–27 schedules and is tested against all 720 amounts in the ATO's sample data.

## Pay slips and files

- **Pay slips** as tidy one-page PDFs with year-to-date totals, leave balances and masked bank and super details, emailed to each person.
- **Files from each pay date:** Single Touch Payroll (Single Touch Lite file), the super clearing house file and an ABA bank file for your bank.

## Leave

- **Annual leave** (four weeks a year) and **personal/carer's leave** (ten days) accrue on hours worked and paid leave; casuals don't accrue.
- **Opening balances** when you start using Jurnl.
- **Leave taken** in a pay, paid out of salary or added to hourly pay; unpaid leave reduces salary.
- **Leave loading** per employee (for example 17.5%), off unless you set it.
- **Leave owed:** hours and dollar value owed to each person, for your liability.

## Final pay

- Choose the last day and the reason: resignation, dismissal, genuine redundancy, ill health and more. Unused leave is paid out and taxed under the ATO's Schedule 7.
- **Payments in lieu of notice, severance and genuine redundancy:** the tax-free part (base plus a year of service), ETP caps and the whole-of-income cap, under Schedule 11. Tested against the ATO's worked examples.
- The employee is marked as past once the final pay is approved; reopening undoes it.

Jurnl doesn't interpret modern awards: you set up the pay items you need, such as overtime rates, allowances and loadings.
