## Bills and suppliers

- **Suppliers** with ABN, contact, payment terms, usual account and tax code, and bank details for paying them.
- **Bills** with GST-inclusive lines, each with its tax code (including capital purchases), and the supplier's PDF attached.
- **Pay by bank file:** select unpaid bills and make one ABA file to upload to your bank, or mark them paid.
- Bills post on their own date (accrual) and move to when paid on cash-basis reports.
- Copies and templates for bills that come every month.

## Bank reconciliation

Bring in your bank statement as CSV or Excel, and Jurnl matches each line to what you've already recorded.

- **Matches** net pay and super (whole runs, a pay date, or some of the people), customer receipts (including combinations of invoices and small differences), bill payments and bank files, and payments recorded on invoices.
- **Bank rules** code the rest: "description contains", direction and amount range, to an account with GST, a memo, or ignore.
- **Split a line** across several accounts or items.
- **Nothing posts until you say so;** unposting puts things back.
- **The running balance** is checked against the statement, and you can lock the books through a quarter once everything agrees.
- **Search** by description, exact amount, whole dollars or a range.

Jurnl works with any bank that exports statements as CSV or Excel and accepts ABA (direct entry) files for payments, which covers the major Australian banks. Statements are imported as files; there are no direct bank feeds.
